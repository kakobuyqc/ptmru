A practical Litbuy shopping guide.

Use a shortlist to make comparisons easier. Keep the exact product variant, available measurements, and unresolved questions together so that a memorable image does not become your only reason to choose an item.

03 / Read the item, not just the title

Three checks for a stronger shortlist.

Shoes: compare sizing information

Check the listing's size chart and any available foot-length guidance. If an insole measurement is provided, consider how it was measured. Similar-looking sneakers can have different shapes and fits.

Clothing: measure something you own

Compare chest width, shoulder width, body length, and sleeve length with a garment that fits you. For pants and shorts, check waist, rise, inseam, and the stated measurement method.

Accessories: inspect dimensions

Look for dimensions, closures, adjustable ranges, and material details. For headwear and jewelry, confirm the relevant sizing information. A brand label alone does not establish material quality or authenticity.

05 / Questions before checkout

Litbuy spreadsheet FAQ.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links, categories, prices, sizing notes, and comparison details. The usefulness of a particular list depends on how accurately its entries are maintained.

How do I use Litbuy product links?

Open the destination listing, confirm that its images and selected variant match your intended item, and review the available measurements and seller information. Follow the purchasing service's current instructions and check compatibility before ordering.

Does a spreadsheet verify product authenticity?

No. A spreadsheet entry, brand name, product image, or quality check photo does not establish authenticity. Use authorized retailers or independently verifiable authentication when authenticity matters.

How should I estimate the total shopping cost?

Add the item price, domestic delivery, applicable service fees, international shipping, and any applicable taxes or duties. Treat the result as an estimate until the relevant costs are confirmed.

Can a product link become outdated?

Yes. Sellers can change listing details, remove variants, adjust prices, or stop offering an item. Recheck the destination listing before making a purchase.

Is this an official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent shopping research guide. Category and browsing links on this page lead to CAFANS.
